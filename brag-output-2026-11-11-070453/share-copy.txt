Private payments and their viewing keys are sealed with ECDH, and quantum computers break it: recorded today, readable later. pq hands keys over with Kyber512 and a zero-knowledge proof made right in your browser, so they stay private for years. Say hi on Sepolia: github.com/mistcash/pq
